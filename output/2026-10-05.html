IEEE Day Bake Sale

October 6, 8:15 AM - IEEE Office

Om Nom Nom! Come celebrate National IEEE Day with us on October 6th & get some delicious treats! IEEE Day is a global celebration held on the first Tuesday of October to mark the first time in 1884 that engineers worldwide gathered to share technical ideas. As such, we'll be holding a bake sale all day in the IEEE Office to celebrate! You can even sign up to bring in baked goods of your own using the link in our Instagram bio!!
